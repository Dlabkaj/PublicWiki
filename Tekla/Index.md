## What this is

Tekla Structures is a Building Information Modeling (BIM) tool by Trimble for creating detailed 3D structural models (steel + concrete), generating fabrication/arrangement drawings, reports, and IFC exports. Models are made of real structural parts (footings, columns, beams, plates, connections), not abstract geometry.
